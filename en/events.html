Network update
IRIS network approved by CNPq		15 July 2026

The Global Network for Resilient, Innovative and Sustainable Infrastructure (IRIS) has been approved under Public Call MCTI/CNPq No. 16/2025, which supports international research, development and innovation projects. Coordinated by Professor Flávio de Andrade Silva at PUC-Rio, IRIS brings together researchers and affiliated laboratories from 23 institutions across South America, North America, Europe and Asia, fostering interdisciplinary and interinstitutional collaboration.

The project has a total approved budget of R$ 531,602.37. It provides mobility fellowships for network researchers and operating funds to support collaborative research activities, including scientific events and experiments. By sharing research infrastructure and data, the network will facilitate joint projects and strengthen connections between academia, industry and society, while promoting the dissemination of findings and the transfer of technology and knowledge.

Learn more about Public Call MCTI/CNPq No. 16/2025.

Event announcement
IRIS Network Launch Workshop		13 October 2026

Researchers and students are invited to attend the IRIS Network Launch Workshop, taking place in person at PUC-Rio in Rio de Janeiro on 14–15 December 2026. The event will introduce the network and provide a forum for scientific exchange among participating researchers and students.

The preliminary programme includes talks on structural health monitoring and infrastructure accident case studies, a student poster session, a roundtable on resilient infrastructure in the face of climate challenges, and a two-part short course on structural health monitoring (SHM). The programme is preliminary and may be updated.
